# 📊 Blinkit Sales & Outlet Analysis Dashboard

✔️ Total Sales: $1M across 8,523 items
✔️ Average Sales per Item: $141
✔️ Customer Rating Analysis: Avg Rating: 4
✔️ Outlet Contribution to Revenue:
- Tier 3: $472K (Largest Share)
- Tier 2: $393K
- Tier 1: $336K
✔️ Item Type Insights:
- Dominant categories: Dairy, Snacks, Frozen Foods
✔️ Best-Performing Outlet Type: Supermarket Type 1 ($787K sales, 5,577 items)
- ✔️ Growth Trends: Outlets established in 2020 saw the highest revenue spike
# 🎯 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides deep insights into Blinkit's sales performance, outlet characteristics, and customer preferences across different retail tiers.
Through data visualization and storytelling, this dashboard helps understand which outlets drive the most revenue, how product categories vary by location, and how business expansion aligns with profitability.
# 🔎 Key Findings & Business Impact
- 🛒 Tier 3 cities outperform others in total revenue, proving higher demand in emerging markets.
- 🥛 Low-fat items sell significantly better in Tier 3 compared to other locations, highlighting health-conscious trends.
- 🛍️ Outlets established in 2020 saw the highest revenue jump, possibly influenced by post-pandemic shifts in shopping behaviors.
- 🏪 Supermarket Type 1 dominates the market, holding over 75% of total sales, indicating strong brand preference and customer loyalty.
# 🔬 Technologies Used
- Power BI → Advanced Data Visualizations
  
# 🚀 Conclusion & Next Steps
This dashboard reveals critical insights for business expansion and product optimization. Blinkit can use these findings to:
✅ Expand more aggressively in Tier 3 locations to capture high-growth opportunities.
✅ Stock more low-fat products in regions where demand is higher.
✅ Leverage supermarket type success to refine branding and pricing strategies.
✅ Analyze post-pandemic growth factors to sustain future sales trends.
